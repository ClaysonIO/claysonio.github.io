Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 3, 2026

Overview

This policy describes how the application (“the App”) handles information when you use it. In short: the App stores your data on your device. We do not operate backend servers that collect or store your personal information for the App’s core functionality.

If you choose to use optional features that connect to third-party cloud or sync services, those services may process data according to their own policies. This document explains what that means for you.

Information the App uses

The App is designed to work primarily with data you create or import while using it—such as notes, entries, files, settings, or other content you add (“Your Data”). That information is stored locally on your device using the device’s storage (for example, app storage or local databases), unless and until you use an optional integration described below.

We do not require you to create an account with us to use the App’s local features, and we do not collect personal identifiers (such as your name, email address, or phone number) from you for those features.

Local data storage

Your Data remains on your device under your control. We do not receive a copy of Your Data simply because you use the App in its default, local-only mode. We cannot access Your Data on your device unless you explicitly share it with us (for example, by emailing support with an attachment—which is entirely voluntary).

If you uninstall the App or clear app data, Your Data may be deleted according to your device and operating system behavior. You are responsible for maintaining your own backups if you need to preserve Your Data.

Optional third-party services and cloud sync

The App may offer optional integrations that let you upload, sync, export, or back up Your Data to services you choose (for example, cloud storage, sync providers, or other platforms). Those features are activated only if you set them up or opt in.

When you use such an integration:

  • The third-party service—not us—receives and processes the data you send according to that service’s terms and privacy policy.
  • We do not control how third parties store, secure, or use your information.
  • You should review the privacy policy of any service you connect before enabling sync or upload.

We are not responsible for the practices of third-party services. Your relationship with those providers is between you and them.

How we share information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share Your Data with advertisers for their own marketing purposes.

Any transmission of Your Data off your device in connection with the App happens only because you enabled a third-party integration or used a feature that explicitly sends data elsewhere, or because you contacted us directly.

Security

Data stored locally on your device is protected by your device’s security settings (such as your lock screen, encryption, and OS updates). We recommend keeping your device software up to date and using a strong device passcode or biometric lock where available.

Because we do not host Your Data on our own servers for core App functionality, we do not apply separate server-side security measures to that local data—but third-party services you connect to may describe their own security practices in their policies.

Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email below and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Effective date” at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy, except where applicable law requires additional notice or consent.
